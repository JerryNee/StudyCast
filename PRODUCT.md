# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Researchers, teaching lab staff, instructors, and demo operators using a Mac to receive, monitor, and record multiple local AirPlay device streams during controlled sessions.

## Product Purpose
StudyCast is a macOS multi-device AirPlay capture desk. It lets one operator run several receiver stations, preview each stream, route per-station monitoring audio, and save recordings or marked clips without breaking the live projection flow.

## Brand Personality
Calm, technical, and session-focused. The interface should feel like reliable lab equipment: direct, readable, and predictable under time pressure.

## Anti-references
It should not feel like consumer broadcast software, a decorative media gallery, or a marketing-heavy AirPlay replacement. Avoid novelty controls that make live-session operation slower or less trustworthy.

## Design Principles
- Keep live-session controls immediately reachable and difficult to confuse.
- Make station state legible at a glance across dense multi-device grids.
- Prefer familiar macOS controls and restrained visual treatment over custom chrome.
- Let projection continue while operators change monitoring, recording, or viewing focus.
- Preserve useful context when a view zooms into one station.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use native macOS control semantics where possible, maintain readable contrast in overlay controls, avoid motion that is required to understand state changes, and keep keyboard and VoiceOver labels meaningful for critical session controls.
